# Netflix-Dashboard
1. Introduction
   
With the rapid growth of digital streaming platforms, data-driven insights play a crucial role in understanding content trends, audience preferences, and platform strategies. Netflix, being one of the world’s leading OTT platforms, offers a vast collection of movies and TV shows across genres, languages, and time periods. This Power BI dashboard has been designed to analyze Netflix content using structured data and interactive visualizations.
The objective of this dashboard is to provide a clear and comprehensive overview of Netflix’s content library by examining content type distribution, release trends over time, IMDb rating patterns, and the popularity of titles based on audience votes. The dashboard supports decision-making by enabling trend analysis, quality assessment, and popularity evaluation in a single consolidated view.

2. Dataset Overview
   
The dataset used for this dashboard contains detailed information about Netflix movies and TV shows. Key attributes include:
•	Title – Name of the movie or TV show
•	Type – Movie or TV Show
•	Release Year – Year of release
•	Age Certification – Content rating
•	Runtime – Duration in minutes
•	IMDb Score – Audience rating
•	IMDb Votes – Number of votes received on IMDb
Before visualization, the dataset was cleaned by handling missing values, especially in IMDb ratings and vote counts, to ensure accurate analysis. The dataset provides a reliable foundation for understanding Netflix’s content strategy and viewer preferences.

3. Dashboard Design and Layout

The dashboard is designed using a 16:9 custom canvas (1280 × 720) to ensure consistency across screens and accurate measurement of visuals. Gridlines and snap-to-grid features were enabled to maintain alignment and professional layout standards.
Key Design Elements:
•	KPI Cards at the top for quick insights
•	Charts and graphs arranged logically for storytelling
•	Consistent color scheme for clarity and readability
•	Interactive filters to allow dynamic exploration of data
This layout ensures that users can quickly grasp high-level insights while also drilling down into detailed analysis.
4. Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

The dashboard displays four major KPIs:
1.	Total Titles – Represents the total number of Netflix titles available in the dataset.
2.	Total Movies – Indicates the total count of movies on Netflix.
3.	Total TV Shows – Shows the total number of TV series available.
4.	Average IMDb Rating – Reflects the overall quality of Netflix content based on audience ratings.
These KPIs provide an instant snapshot of the platform’s content volume and overall quality.

5. Content Type Distribution Analysis

The Movies vs TV Shows donut chart illustrates the proportion of movies and TV shows available on Netflix. The visualization clearly shows that movies form a larger share of the platform’s content library compared to TV shows.
Insights:
•	Netflix focuses more on movies to cater to a wider audience.
•	TV shows, although fewer, often contribute to long-term viewer engagement.
This analysis helps understand Netflix’s content acquisition and production strategy.

6. Content Released Over the Years

The Content Released Over Years chart displays the trend of Netflix content releases across different years. This visualization highlights a significant increase in content production in recent years.
Insights:
•	Rapid growth after 2015 indicates Netflix’s aggressive expansion strategy.
•	A surge in original content production reflects competition in the OTT market.
This trend analysis is useful for identifying growth phases and forecasting future content strategies.

7. IMDb Rating Distribution Analysis

The IMDb Rating Distribution chart shows how Netflix titles are rated by viewers. Most titles fall within the mid-to-high rating range.
Insights:
•	Majority of content is rated between 6 and 8, indicating good overall quality.
•	Very few titles have extremely low ratings, suggesting quality control in content selection.
This analysis helps assess audience satisfaction and content performance.

8. Top 10 Most Popular Titles

The Top 10 Most Popular Titles bar chart ranks titles based on IMDb vote count. High vote counts indicate strong viewer engagement and popularity.
Insights:
•	Popular titles attract significantly higher engagement.
•	These titles often drive subscriptions and platform visibility.
This visualization is valuable for identifying flagship content and understanding audience preferences.

9. Interactivity Features

The dashboard includes interactive features such as:
•	Hover tooltips for detailed values
•	Filters for content type and year
•	Dynamic updates across all visuals
These features enhance user experience and allow customized analysis without modifying the underlying data.

10. Conclusion

The Power BI Netflix dashboard successfully transforms raw data into meaningful insights. It provides a holistic view of Netflix’s content library by combining volume analysis, trend analysis, quality evaluation, and popularity measurement.
This dashboard can be effectively used by:
•	Business analysts for strategic insights
•	Content managers for decision-making
•	Students for academic projects and presentations
Overall, the dashboard demonstrates the power of data visualization in understanding digital entertainment platforms and supports informed, data-driven decisions.
